# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex identity and orientation, religion, or belief.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, safe,
and welcoming community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for
include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual
  advances or attention
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic
  address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported by opening an issue or contacting the project team at
[email protected]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to
the situation. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality
with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with
this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor
for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or offensive.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not
limited to repositories, issue trackers, chat rooms, and mailing lists, as
well as in any public events held as part of the project. Individuals may
also be held accountable for unacceptable behavior occurring outside of
project spaces when such behavior has the potential to adversely affect
the project or its contributors.
